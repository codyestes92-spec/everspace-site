Installation and dismantling

Show up to a finished booth.

Our fully insured crew sets up your booth before doors open and takes it down after the show closes. You walk in to a finished booth and walk out without lifting a crate.

Get a free quoteSee booth sizes

What we do

  • Setup on schedule, before the show opens
  • Coordination of electrical, lighting and AV hookups
  • A final walkthrough with you before the show starts
  • Dismantling, packing and crating after the show
  • Coordination with show labor and the show's general contractor
  • A condition report on anything that needs repair

Why it matters

Setup is where shows go wrong. A crew that knows your booth gets it up faster, safer and right the first time.

Good for

  • Teams flying in the day before
  • Larger booths that need a trained crew
  • Anyone who would rather not spend show day on a ladder
